Built from the ground up in Chhattisgarh.

TruBuild Innovations was founded in Raipur in 2017 with a single goal: to deliver pre-engineered buildings faster, cheaper, and more reliably than the market offered. Not as a trading house sourcing from distant fabricators, but as a manufacturer with its own steel, its own machines, and its own erection crews.

The Dhamtari facility was built to serve Central India — a region where large-scale agro, industrial, and manufacturing construction was growing fast but good PEB contractors were few. Seven years later, 150+ structures stand as proof of the model.

We are not the biggest PEB company in India. We are the most accountable one in the region we serve.
