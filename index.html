What We Build

Everything under one roof.

PEB Fabrication
Columns, rafters, trusses, crane beams — all primary and secondary steel components manufactured in-house at our Dhamtari facility.
Purlins & Secondary
Z & C Purlins from 100–300mm. Custom lengths, high-strength galvanised steel — wall girts, bracing, and eave members.
Profile Sheets
Galvalume, Galvanized, and Colour-Coated sheets. 0.45–0.60mm TCT. Multiple rib profiles for roof and wall cladding.
Insulation Systems
Bubble, Rockwool, and Glasswool insulation. Ridge and turbo ventilators, gutters, flashings, and all weather accessories.
Turnkey Construction
Foundation to handover — civil works, structural erection, cladding, and finishing. Single-vendor accountability throughout.
Design Engineering
Structural analysis in STAAD Pro. 3D connection detailing in TEKLA Structures. All designs to IS 800 standards.
